Privacy Policy

Security Technology Organizer · Effective November 3, 2026

Security Technology Organizer ("STO", "we", "us") is a tracking system for security teams at hotels and commercial facilities. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account information — your company name, your name, your work email, and a securely hashed password (we never store your password in plain text).
  • Your company’s operational data — the records your team enters into the system, such as emergency fob, radio, and camera inventories, inspection entries, and audit information.
  • Basic technical data — information needed to operate the service, such as session information stored on your device.

How we use it

  • To provide the service: your company’s workspace, tracking tools, reports, and audits.
  • To secure accounts, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot problems.
  • To communicate with the account administrator about the service, including trial and subscription status.

Your company’s data stays your company’s

Each company’s data is stored in its own separate workspace. No other customer can see it. We do not sell your data, and we do not use your operational data for advertising.

Service providers

We use reputable cloud infrastructure providers to host the service and store data. They process data only to provide the service to us.

Data retention and deletion

Your administrator can delete the company account and all of its data at any time from inside the app (More → Delete account). Deletion is permanent. When a trial ends, data is retained so a subscribing company can pick up where it left off; contact us to have it deleted sooner.

Security

Passwords are stored as cryptographic hashes, data travels over encrypted connections, and each company’s workspace is isolated from every other company’s.

Children

STO is a workplace tool and is not directed to children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version here and update the effective date.
