The founder's story is the product's story.

Every security team knows the drill: the fobs are on a spreadsheet, the radios are on a clipboard, the camera list lives in somebody's email — and when something goes missing, everybody scrambles. Jeremy lived that scramble, and decided the fix wasn't another spreadsheet. It was one system, built by the person who actually does the work.

So he built it — module by module, in the field: emergency fob tracking with warranty clocks, radio tracking with the repair pipeline, camera tracking with GPS locations, package logging, inspections, team chat, and vendor portals. Each feature earned its place by surviving real shifts, real audits, and a real team that does not like change.

Today the suite runs the security technology operation across 11 hotel properties — one login, every record in the cloud, the whole team looking at the same live data. Jeremy still runs it on shift, every day. When a customer calls, they don't get a sales rep. They get the founder — the same person who built it, uses it, and answers for it.

He builds under NDL Systems Tech, named for his father — the standard behind every build: it has to look and work like someone paid $50,000 for it.
