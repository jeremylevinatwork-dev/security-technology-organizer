THE PROBLEM

Paper sheets and one person's memory are not a system

  • Emergency fobs walk away and nobody knows who had them last — at $181 per fob, that adds up fast.
  • Radios get lost, sit broken in a drawer, or can't be accounted for at audit time.
  • Cameras go down and stay down — found weeks later, usually after an incident.
  • Packages pile up at the dock with a paper log nobody can search.
  • Vendor visits leave no record of what the technician actually did.
THE SUITE

Six modules. One login. Every property.

Each module stands on its own; together they are the whole security technology operation, visible at a glance.

🔐

Secure Alert — Emergency Fobs

Every emergency fob by property: who it's assigned to, status, warranty clock, and cost tracking at $181 each — with per-property cost reports ready to print. Mistyped an ID or tag number? Fix it right on the fob's page — duplicates are refused, every correction is logged, and the tag-remake list prints with just the numbers, big and clean.

📻

Radio Tracking

Every radio by serial number — in the field, in repair, retired — with a full handling log on every move and a repair pipeline your vendor can see. The Need Attention count flags only radios on hand that need a move — radios already at the repair shop don't cry wolf.

📷

Camera Tracking

Every camera's status, service tickets and vendor quotes, with photos pinned to their exact GPS location on a printable map sheet.

📦

Package Tracking

Scan the shipping label and the carrier, recipient and hotel fill themselves in. Daily delivery sheets per property — no more paper log.

📋

Daily Inspections

The team's property inspection log — searchable, printable by month, exportable. No more clipboards.

💬

Team Chat & Client Portal

The security team talks in one place, and hotel staff open their own fob-repair tickets that route to your desk for approval.

🧾

Service Visit Log

Every vendor visit documented — SecurAlert, radio and camera technicians — what they did, what they replaced, on the record.

🚛

FedEx Shipments

Every equipment shipment in one place — FedEx tracking that taps straight to the carrier, shipped dates, the units in each box, and received status. Label and receipt files attach to the shipment, and the list prints in one tap.

🏨

Built for Multi-Property Teams

A director sees all locations at once; each property sees its own. Roles and approvals keep the right people in the right data.
